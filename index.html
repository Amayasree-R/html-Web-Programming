Welcome to the ECE Department

The Department of Electronics and Communication Engineering started functioning in 1998. It trains students in electronics, signal processing, communication systems and embedded design.

The department is accredited by NBA and has well-qualified faculty with industry and research experience.

Academic Programmes

We offer a 4-year B.Tech in ECE and a 2-year M.Tech in VLSI Design.

Subjects offered:

  • Electronic Circuits
  • Digital Signal Processing
  • Analog and Digital Communication
  • Microprocessors and Microcontrollers
  • VLSI Design
  • Electromagnetic Theory
